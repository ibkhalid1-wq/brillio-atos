# ATOS Flow — Executive Demo Script

~20 minutes on a reset "Flow Pilot" programme. Prep: two sponsor/stakeholder
transcripts (~150 words each) and one document snippet in a notes file; a
second browser window; a wide screen (>1380px) so the spine pointer shows.

## Act 1 — Conversations in (5 min)

1. **Open the app.** Lands on the Flow canvas — six movements on one spine, a
   pulsing **"Needs you"** pointer on Frame.
   > "ATOS never asks where to start — it points."
2. **Open Frame.** The meeting kit is open: 1 Engage the sponsor · 2 Run the
   script · 3 Capture · 4 Close the loop. The questions are the missing facts.
   > "No blank forms. If the input is a conversation, ATOS hands you the conversation."
3. **Paste the sponsor transcript into Capture.** The gate checklist ticks
   "Sponsor conversation on record" by itself; the quote appears as evidence;
   artifact criteria flip to "regenerate — evidence changed".
   > "The gate is a checklist that ticks itself — and un-ticks when the ground moves."
4. **Generate the Transformation Charter.** Show the generation theater; then
   click the title — the reading pane opens the full document with
   **Grounded in** (CiTO-typed sources, JSON-LD one tap away).
   > "Every claim traces to who said it. Audit-grade by construction."

## Act 2 — ATOS works the loop (7 min)

5. **Generate the Discovery Kit**, then in Listen click **Create response
   links**. Open a link in the second window: the public portal, no login.
   > "This is ATOS conducting the interview itself."
6. **Answer as the stakeholder, send.** Back in the app — no reload — the dock
   badge lights; the **Inbox** holds the response in quarantine.
   > "External input never touches the record until a human says so."
7. **Ingest it.** Roster flips to Heard, coverage moves, the Listen gate goes
   green. Show the follow-up kit turning amber ("N gaps to close") — schedule
   it, or send it as a link.
   > "If a meeting missed something, ATOS writes the follow-up and runs it."
8. **Open the Domain Ontology** — adopted standard-mapping chips
   (Quote ⇢ Quotation) linking to schema.org.
   > "The client's language, grounded in the industry's — one decision."

## Act 3 — Systems out (6 min)

9. **Generate the Architecture Strategy, then the Blueprint.** Its track plan
   arrives in the Inbox as **Adopt the track plan**; confirm; switch to
   **Tracks**: workstreams with dependency chips and lead stakeholders.
   > "One confirm turned architecture into an operating board."
10. **Record a show pass** on a track; point at the acceptance rule
    ("pass 2 · accepted"). Demos travel by link too; verdicts land in the
    Inbox; one confirm updates the tour ledger AND the track record.
11. **Ship: compile the plan** — six lanes from the blueprint. Tick the
    validation and cutover items; the gate flips green: "Eval suite green and
    cutover executed."
12. **Mission:** halt the programme (banner appears; runs refuse with a 423 on
    the trail), resume; show budget bars over real token spend.
    > "Every lever is governed, every pull is on the record."

## Close (2 min)

13. **Portfolio** (days-to-demo, open decisions, stalls per programme) →
    **Pulse** (the steering view) → end on the **Inbox quiet state**:
    "Nothing needs you right now."
    > "That sentence is the product: ATOS works between conversations — you're
    > only interrupted for judgment."
